# Exim

Exim is a flexible mail transfer agent (MTA) with a powerful and expressive routing and access-control configuration language. It is the default MTA on many Unix-like systems and is well suited to complex mail policies and large deployments. Its extensive conditionals give administrators precise control over how mail is accepted, routed and delivered.

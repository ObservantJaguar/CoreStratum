# Funkwhale

Funkwhale is a federated music streaming platform that lets you host and listen to your own audio library without depending on a commercial service. Through ActivityPub federation, users can follow libraries, artists and playlists across instances, and discover music shared by others. Funkwhale provides streaming, pod management and an open API for integration.

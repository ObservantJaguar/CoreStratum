# PeerTube

PeerTube is a decentralized video hosting platform that distributes video playback across participating servers to reduce bandwidth pressure. It federates through ActivityPub, allowing instances to subscribe to and share channels from across the network. PeerTube provides a web player, live streaming, adaptive bitrate and full administrator control over content policies.

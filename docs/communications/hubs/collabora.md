# Collabora

Collabora Online is a self-hosted office suite for browser-based, real-time collaboration on documents, spreadsheets and presentations. It builds on the LibreOffice technology core, providing strong format compatibility while working within existing file-hosting and collaboration hubs. Collabora is a mature choice for organizations that want a self-hosted, private alternative to commercial office suites.

# Conversations

Conversations is the reference XMPP client for Android, notable for its clean design and strong security focus. It supports end-to-end encryption with OMEMO, voice and video calls, and a full range of chat features, making it a leading choice for privacy-conscious messaging. Its reliability and default-open behavior make it the standard way to connect Android users to a self-hosted XMPP server.

# Jitsi Meet

Jitsi Meet is an open-source video conferencing platform that runs entirely in the browser without requiring client installation. Its media routing nodes (Jitsi Videobridge and Jicofo) distribute video streams efficiently among participants, enabling scalable group calls. Self-hosting Jitsi Meet provides a fully controlled alternative to commercial conferencing services.

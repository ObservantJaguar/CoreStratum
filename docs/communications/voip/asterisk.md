# Asterisk

Asterisk is a widely used open-source software PBX that provides flexible logic for managing and routing calls. It supports a broad range of voice, video and data protocols, and its dialplan lets administrators build everything from a simple phone system to complex IVR and call centers. Asterisk runs on commodity hardware and is the foundation of most self-hosted telephone exchanges.

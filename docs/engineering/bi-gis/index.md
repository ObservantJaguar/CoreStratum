# BI/GIS — Business Intelligence and GIS

BI/GIS covers open-source platforms for business analytics and geographic information systems. The category includes dashboard and reporting tools for business intelligence, report generators, and geospatial analysis systems built around spatial databases.

## Business Intelligence

- [Metabase](metabase.md) — open-source analytics and dashboard platform
- [Apache Superset](apache-superset.md) — modern data exploration and visualization platform
- [Pentaho](pentaho.md) — business analytics and data integration platform
- [JasperReports](jasperreports.md) — open-source reporting library

## GIS

- [QGIS](qgis.md) — full-featured open-source GIS desktop application
- [PostGIS](postgis.md) — spatial database extender for PostgreSQL
- [GRASS GIS](grass-gis.md) — geospatial data management and analysis suite

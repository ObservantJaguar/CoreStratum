# CRM — Customer Relationship Management

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) covers open-source systems for managing interactions with customers and prospects, including lead tracking, sales pipelines, and customer support.

- [SuiteCRM](suitecrm.md) — open-source CRM system
- [EspoCRM](espocrm.md) — lightweight open-source CRM platform

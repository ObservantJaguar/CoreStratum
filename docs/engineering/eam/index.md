# EAM/ITAM — Asset and IT Management

EAM/ITAM covers open-source platforms for tracking physical assets, IT equipment, software licenses, and infrastructure inventory, as well as managing service and maintenance processes. The category includes dedicated asset management systems, data-center infrastructure documentation tools, and hardware inventory platforms.

- [Snipe-IT](snipe-it.md) — IT asset management with license and consumable tracking
- [OpenMAINT](openmaint.md) — asset and maintenance management platform
- [NetBox](netbox.md) — infrastructure documentation, IPAM, and DCIM tool
- [PartKeepr](partkeepr.md) — electronic component inventory management
- [OCS Inventory](ocs-inventory.md) — automatic hardware and software inventory
- [GLPI](glpi.md) — IT asset management and service desk platform

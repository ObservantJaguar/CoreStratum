# ECM/EDMS — Content and Document Management

ECM/EDMS covers open-source platforms for managing enterprise content and electronic documents. The category includes comprehensive enterprise content management systems and focused electronic document management systems with OCR, workflow, and audit capabilities.

- [Alfresco](alfresco.md) — open-source enterprise content management platform
- [Mayan EDMS](mayan-edms.md) — open-source electronic document management system

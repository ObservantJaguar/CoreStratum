# ERP — Enterprise Resource Planning

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) covers open-source platforms for managing and integrating core business processes such as finance, accounting, inventory, manufacturing, and human resources. The category includes modular all-in-one suites and scalable systems built on open standards.

- [Odoo](odoo.md) — modular open-source business management suite
- [ERPNext](erpnext.md) — all-in-one open-source ERP platform
- [Tryton](tryton.md) — modular enterprise resource planning framework
- [iDempiere](idempiere.md) — scalable ERP business suite

# SCM/POS — Supply Chain and Commerce

SCM/POS covers open-source systems for managing supply chains, warehouse operations, and points of sale. The category includes inventory and replenishment platforms, specialized warehouse management systems, and retail and hospitality point-of-sale applications.

- [OpenBoxes](openboxes.md) — supply chain and inventory management
- [myWMS](mywms.md) — warehouse management system
- [UniCenta](unicenta.md) — open-source retail point-of-sale system
- [SambaPOS](sambapos.md) — solution for restaurant and cafe automation

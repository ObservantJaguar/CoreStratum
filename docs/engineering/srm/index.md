# SRM — Collaboration and Supply Management

SRM covers open-source tools for team collaboration and supplier relationship management. The category includes team messaging and chat platforms, collaborative document editing tools, secure note-taking environments, and supply-chain and purchasing systems.

## Collaboration

- [Mattermost](mattermost.md) — open-source team collaboration and messaging platform
- [Zulip](zulip.md) — open-source threaded team chat platform
- [Etherpad](etherpad.md) — real-time collaborative document editor
- [CryptPad](cryptpad.md) — encrypted collaboration and note-taking suite

## Supply and Supplier Management

- [Apache OFBiz](apache-ofbiz.md) — open-source enterprise resource planning framework
- [Odoo Purchase](odoo-purchase.md) — purchasing module of the Odoo ERP suite

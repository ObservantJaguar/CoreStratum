# Learning Paths

A learning path answers the question "how do I become an X?" - a structured self-study roadmap for a specific IT role. Each path goes from general knowledge to concrete tools, and finally to the typical stack requested in real vacancies.

## The general shape of every path

1. **Role overview** - what the specialist does day to day (link to the [IT Roles](../roles/index.md) card).
2. **Foundations** - the theory and protocols a candidate must understand.
3. **Core tools** - the software to learn, grouped by responsibility domains.
4. **Practice** - the [guides](../guides/index.md) and [recipes](../guides/recipes/index.md) to do hands-on.
5. **Typical vacancy stack** - what actually shows up in job postings, so you know when you are ready to apply.

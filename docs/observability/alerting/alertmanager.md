# Alertmanager

Alertmanager is the alerting component of the Prometheus stack that receives alert notifications from Prometheus and other compatible sources. It handles grouping, deduplication, inhibition, silence, and routing to notification channels such as email, webhooks, Slack, and PagerDuty, reducing noise and delivering alerts to the right teams.

Official documentation: <https://prometheus.io/docs/alerting/latest/alertmanager/>

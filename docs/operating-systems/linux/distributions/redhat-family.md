# Red Hat Family

The Red Hat family covers distributions built on the RPM package ecosystem and the Red Hat enterprise platform lineage. These systems are widely deployed in datacenters, clouds and enterprise environments.

The family is organized around major enterprise distributions and their community rebuilds:

- **RHEL (Red Hat Enterprise Linux)** — commercial enterprise distribution.
- **Fedora** — upstream innovation platform.
- **CentOS Stream** — rolling preview of RHEL.
- **Rocky Linux** — community RHEL rebuild.
- **AlmaLinux** — enterprise-grade RHEL clone.
- **Oracle Linux** — RHEL-compatible distribution.

Dedicated pages for individual Red Hat distributions are planned for a future revision.

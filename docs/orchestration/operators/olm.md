# Operator Lifecycle Manager

The Operator Lifecycle Manager (OLM) helps manage the lifecycle of operators and their custom resources on Kubernetes clusters. It automates the installation, upgrade, and RBAC of operators by inspecting their metadata, and coordinates with OperatorHub as a registry of published operators.

Official site: <https://olm.operatorframework.io>

# IT Roles

IT job titles are often copied from job boards without a clear understanding of what the role actually requires. This section provides structured profiles of the main IT roles: what each specialist does, what falls outside their responsibility, and the tool stack that actually shows up again and again in real vacancies.

Each profile covers:

- **Scope** — responsibilities and areas of ownership.
- **Out of scope** — what is explicitly NOT part of the role (useful when hiring).
- **Typical stack** — the tools that recur across real job postings.
- **Adjacent roles and career path** — how the role borders on others and where it leads.

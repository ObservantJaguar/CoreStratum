# ZFS Snapshots

ZFS snapshots are instantaneous, atomic read-only copies of a filesystem or volume at a given point in time. They consume minimal space initially, support rollback and cloning, and can be sent incrementally to remote systems for efficient replication.

Official handbook: <https://docs.freebsd.org/en/books/handbook/zfs/>

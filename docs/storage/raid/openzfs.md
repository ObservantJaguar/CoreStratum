# OpenZFS

OpenZFS is the open-source port of the ZFS filesystem and volume manager. Its RAID-Z levels provide checksummed, parity-based redundancy combined with snapshots, compression, and copy-on-write storage, offering an alternative to traditional mdadm arrays.
